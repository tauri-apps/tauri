Add more predefined menu items `PredefinedMenuItem::section_header`, `paste_and_match_style`, `delete`, `zoom` (an alias for `maximize`, named after the macOS window action), `actual_size`, `zoom_in`, `zoom_out`, `start_speaking`, `stop_speaking`, `start_dictation` and `emoji_and_symbols`. They are also available in JavaScript as the `SectionHeader`, `PasteAndMatchStyle`, `Delete`, `Zoom`, `ActualSize`, `ZoomIn`, `ZoomOut`, `StartSpeaking`, `StopSpeaking`, `StartDictation` and `EmojiAndSymbols` values of `PredefinedMenuItemOptions.item`.
